A better study rhythm

Read it. Try it.
Make it stick.

Getting something wrong is useful.
It tells you where to go next.

  1. 01

    Get the idea.

    Start with focused notes that explain the concept, without making the topic feel bigger than it is.

    Read notes ↗
  2. 02

    Give it a go.

    Answer questions and see the reasoning. Find the specific idea that needs another look.

    Practise questions ↗
  3. 03

    Work it out.

    Revisit an explanation, ask someone, then try again. Understanding takes practice, and that’s okay.

    Ask the community ↗
